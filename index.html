When will it arrive?

Orders are dispatched within 1–2 working days with free tracked delivery, and usually arrive in 7–12 days. We email your tracking number when it ships.

How does the hologram work?

The fan's arms are lined with LEDs. As they spin, the LEDs light up in exact patterns, and your eyes blend them into one picture that seems to float in the air.

What can I show on it?

Photos, videos and logos in MP4, AVI, JPG or PNG format, uploaded over WiFi from the app on your phone or from a computer. Pictures with a black background look best, because black areas stay invisible and the subject appears to float.

Can I adjust it?

Yes. You can change the brightness and rotate your picture to the right angle from the app.

Does it work in daylight?

It works indoors in normal light and looks most striking in dim rooms, shop windows at night, and evening events. Very bright direct sunlight washes it out.

Is it safe?

The blades spin fast, so mount it out of reach of children, pets and people walking past, and switch it off before touching it.

Which plug do I need?

Choose UK, EU, US or AU when you order and we'll send the matching power adapter. It works on any mains voltage from 100 to 240V.
